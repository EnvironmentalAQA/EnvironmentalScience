Question 1010 marks · Atmospheric pollution: smoke, acid rain, NOx, hydrocarbons, CO, smogs · spec 3.4.3.2.1-3.4.3.2.5 · Genn pp. 245-254 · AIR-07
[9 marks]10.1Explain how the properties of smoke and of the gases involved in acid rain lead to their environmental effects, and evaluate the effectiveness of the methods used to control them.
[1 mark]10.2State one effect of tropospheric ozone on plants.

Indicative content - credit any of the following (and other relevant, accurate points):
  • Smoke: solid particulates (PM10, PM5, PM1) from incomplete combustion; small particles stay suspended, penetrate lungs; toxic chemicals (acids, phenols, metals) carried on particles; high albedo cools climate; blocks light reducing photosynthesis; damages buildings
  • Smoke smogs form under temperature inversions in valleys (London 1952, 12 000 deaths); synergism with SO2
  • Controls: Clean Air Act 1956 (smokeless zones), smokeless fuels / gas / electricity, coal treatment, electrostatic precipitators, cyclones, bag filters, scrubbers, DPFs, better combustion; smoke smogs have largely been eliminated in UK cities
  • Acid rain gases: SO2 (coal combustion, smelting sulfide ores) oxidised by ozone to SO3 - sulfuric acid; NOx (high temperature combustion) - nitric acid; HCl from PVC incineration; reactive gases travel long distances (transboundary pollution)
  • Effects: pH denatures proteins / enzymes in exposed cells (gills, eggs, root hairs, stomata); dissolves exoskeletons; leaches Ca and Mg then mobilises toxic Al and Pb; corrodes metals, dissolves limestone; severity depends on bedrock (limestone neutralises)
  • Controls: fuel desulfurisation (coal washing, hydrodesulfurisation, low-sulfur ship fuels), dry and wet FGD, fluidised bed combustion, catalytic converters, urea sprays; switching from coal to gas / renewables
  • Evaluation: SO2 emissions in Europe have fallen dramatically and lake pH is recovering; NOx harder to control because of growing vehicle numbers; catalytic converters need lead-free fuel and warm engines; FGD is costly and produces wastes / gypsum; controlling primary pollutants also prevents secondary pollutants (ozone, PANs)
  • Judgement on relative success of controls for particulates vs SO2 vs NOx
/ 9
10.2 1 mark
Any 1 from the points below - 1 mark each, maximum 1.
  • 1Reduces growth rates of trees / crops
  • 1Damages leaf cuticles (allowing SO2 to cause more damage)
/ 1
