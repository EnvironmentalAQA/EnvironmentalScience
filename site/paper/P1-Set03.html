  • Critical Pathway Analysis: predicting movement of mobile persistent pollutants (state of effluent, wind, currents, geology, pH / oxygen and solubility, bioaccumulation, food sources, half-lives) so monitoring focuses on sites at risk; mainly radioactive discharges; checked by sampling
